1 · Forward kinematics (numbers → space)The FR3 arm's geometry is public: how long each link is and how each joint turns. Chaining the 7 joint angles through it gives the exact position of every joint and of the gripper. That is the 3D model below.
2 · Inverse kinematics (space → numbers)The reverse: "put the gripper here, at this angle" → which 7 joint angles? A gripper pose is 6 numbers, the arm has 7 joints, so there is one joint too many: infinitely many answers.
3 · One rule → one answerWe recalculate the recording from the gripper path alone. A: no extra rule (just "move as little as possible"): the gripper path is perfect, but the arm drifts into other postures. B: add one rule (joint 7 follows its recorded angle): 6 equations, 6 unknowns, a single answer — the original trajectory comes back.
