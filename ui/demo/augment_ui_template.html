Box grid player

1 · Build the table once (offline)For every 5 cm box position on the cart, inverse kinematics (step 2) bends the one recorded handover so the gripper reaches that spot. Each feasible position gets its own ready-made trajectory: a lookup table (LUT). Red cells: the arm cannot get there.
2 · Find the box with one cameraThe head camera's position and lens are known, so every pixel on the cart maps to one spot on the table. The box is detected in the picture and converted to "dx, dz cm from the recorded spot".
3 · Look up, don't solveThe spot is snapped to the nearest grid cell and that cell's trajectory is played. No solving at run time: the camera picks a row in the table. Try it: drag the box in the picture.

Place the box in the camera image

Drag the box (or click anywhere on the cart). It snaps to the nearest feasible grid cell and that cell's trajectory loads on the right. Head camera, training video, episode 0 at 1.0 s.
