1 · What was recordedA scripted robot did the handover 84 times. Every 1/30 s the recorder saved three camera pictures and the robot's numbers.
2 · The numbers16 values per moment: each arm's 7 joint angles (radians) and its gripper opening (1 = open, 0 = closed). "State" is where the robot is; "action" is where it is told to go next.
3 · What is missingThere are no positions in space: no "the hand is here". From this matrix alone you see angles changing, not where the gripper goes. That needs the robot's geometry (step 2).
