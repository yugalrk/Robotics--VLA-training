How we split each recording into sub-tasks

A handover recording is one 75-second motion. We cut it into 5 named steps so the model gets a short, specific instruction for every frame ("Grasp the box with the right gripper") instead of one long one. The cuts come only from the robot's own numbers, not from looking at the video.

3How one cut is placed

1 · MeasureEvery frame: is each arm moving (joints faster than 0.02 rad/s for at least 0.1 s)? Did a gripper start closing or opening?
2 · Find the eventEach new step starts with a clear event, e.g. "the left gripper starts opening" or "the right arm starts moving after it grasped".
3 · Step back to the pauseJust before every event the robot waits with both arms still. Find where that pause began.
4 · Cut in the middlePlace the boundary in the middle of the pause. If it is a few frames off, nothing visible changes, because the robot isn't moving.

4Checks on every episode

  • The 4 cuts are in order, and each one sits inside a still pause
  • Each step contains its gripper event (e.g. "grasp (right)" contains the right gripper closing)
  • Grippers make sense at each cut: both hold at the handover; only the right holds after the release
  • No step is much longer or shorter than the same step in other episodes
  • Snapshots at the cuts were looked at by eye, and the saved dataset was re-loaded to confirm every frame returns the right text
